TokenCounter v1.0.0 · zero dependencies

Every token, counted.

A small, dependency-free widget that shows your token count with a verified/estimated badge, model, and cost — dropped into any static site with one <link> and one <script>. No framework, no build step, no network calls.

Live demo

02

Why it exists

Zero dependencies

No framework, no runtime, no network calls. One file in, widgets out.

Tiny by design

Roughly 13 KB of vanilla JavaScript plus a scoped stylesheet. Nothing to bundle.

Theme-aware

Follows your host's data-theme or the OS, live, and re-paints the moment either changes.

Counts up

A restrained easeOutCubic count-up on first render — and it honors reduced-motion.

Universal (UMD)

Works as a browser global, CommonJS module, or AMD. Your choice.

Responsive

Sits inline anywhere and collapses gracefully on small screens.

03

Usage

<!-- index.html -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="token-counter.css">
<div id="counter"></div>
<script src="token-counter.js"></script>
<script>
  TokenCounter.init({
    target: "#counter",
    tokens: 2841921,
    verified: true,
    estimated: false,
    model: "GPT-5",
    cost: 42.18,
    theme: "auto"  // "auto" | "light" | "dark"
  });
</script>
